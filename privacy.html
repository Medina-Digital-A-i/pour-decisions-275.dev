How we handle your info

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · Pour Decisions · 359 Northern Blvd, Albany, NY 12204

Plain-English version: we collect what we need to run your account and rewards, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it any time.

Who we are

Pour Decisions is a juice bar at 359 Northern Blvd, Albany, NY 12204. This policy covers pourdecisionsjuicebar.com and the Pour Decisions app on that site. Questions: pourdecisionsalb@gmail.com or (838) 261-9233.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: name, email, password (stored only as a salted hash — we can't read it), and optionally your phone number and birthday (month and day).
  • Rewards activity: order requests you place through the site, purchases staff mark as paid, Pour Pass stamps, points, prizes and monthly spins.
  • Preferences: whether you opted in to emails about new drinks, deals and Pour Pass perks, and any event RSVPs.
  • Basic usage data: which screens are viewed and how often, so we can improve the site. No advertising trackers, no cross-site tracking.

How we use it

  • Run your account and the Pour Pass rewards program.
  • Prepare order requests and let staff match them to a paid purchase at the register.
  • Send you emails about drops, deals and events — only if you opted in. Every email has an unsubscribe option, and you can switch marketing off in your account at any time.
  • Keep the site working and improve it.

Payments

Payments are taken at the register or through our Clover point-of-sale system. We never see or store your full card number on this site.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the services that run the site — hosting and data storage (Netlify), payments (Clover), and email delivery — and only as needed to operate. We'll disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies & storage

The site stores a sign-in token, your cart and your display preferences in your browser so you stay signed in and your cart survives a refresh. Signing out clears them.

Your choices

  • Update your name, phone, birthday and marketing preference under You in the app.
  • Change your password under You → Change password. Forgot it? Ask at the counter — staff can issue a temporary one.
  • Delete your account and data: ask at the counter or email pourdecisionsalb@gmail.com. We'll remove it within 30 days, except records we must keep for accounting.

Kids

The site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, note it in the app.
